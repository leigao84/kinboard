# Sample photos

The five pictures bundled with the Photo Jigsaw as its default set —
what the picker shows until a board's own photos are dealt in. Every
one is in the public domain; each entry names the work, where it came
from, and the basis for that status. All five were resized to a
1600 px long edge for the board.

1. **Earth, from Apollo 17** (sample-1.jpg) — "The Earth seen from
   Apollo 17" (the Blue Marble), NASA / Apollo 17 crew, 1972.
   Public domain: a work of the U.S. federal government.
   https://images.nasa.gov/details/as17-148-22727

2. **Sierra Nevada, California** (sample-2.jpg) — "Manzanar from
   Guard Tower, view west (Sierra Nevada in background), Manzanar
   Relocation Center, California", Ansel Adams for the U.S.
   Department of the Interior, 1943. Library of Congress: "No known
   restrictions on reproduction." (The scan's film borders were
   cropped.)
   https://www.loc.gov/item/2002695970/

3. **The Grand Canyon** (sample-3.jpg) — "Grand Canyon, Arizona",
   Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division. "No known
   restrictions on publication."
   https://www.loc.gov/item/2011630363/

4. **Portland Head Light** (sample-4.jpg) — "Portland Head Light,
   Portland, Maine", Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs
   Division. "No known restrictions on publication."
   https://www.loc.gov/item/2011635090/

5. **A sea turtle** (sample-5.jpg) — photograph by Aleksei Bakulin,
   via StockSnap. Released under CC0, the public-domain dedication.
   https://stocksnap.io/photo/diving-snorkel-ELADKTWHHU
